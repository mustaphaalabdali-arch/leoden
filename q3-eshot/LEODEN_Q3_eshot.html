A New Chapter for Leoden dentsu

There’s a lot happening at Leoden dentsu, and we’re excited to share it with you.

Our Strategic partnership with dentsu marks an important step in our journey, bringing together our understanding of regional markets with the expertise, resources, and reach of a global network. It’s a partnership that opens new opportunities for our team, our clients, and the work we do together.

And what better way to mark this chapter than by sharing what we’ve been up to?

Welcome to the first edition of The Quarter at Leoden dentsu, where we’ll be sharing the partnerships, projects, milestones, and moments that have shaped our journey over the past few months.

 

July through September brought another period of growth across Leoden dentsu. From welcoming new leadership and expanding our client portfolio to launching new experiences, delivering campaigns and bringing major events to life, our teams continued to make their mark across the region.

Here’s a look at what shaped the past three months

 

Marize Sami Joins Leoden Egypt as Managing Director

 

Leoden MENA welcomed Marize Sami as Managing Director, bringing more than 20 years of experience in advertising, strategic communications and agency leadership.

An AUC graduate, Marize has held leadership roles at Dentsu Creative, FP7/CAI, Leo Burnett and BBDO, working across FMCG, banking, automotive and real estate. Her work has been recognized at Cannes, Dubai Lynx, the Effies, African Crystal, Global Crystal and D&AD.

With extensive industry experience and a strong creative background, Marize will lead Leoden MENA’s operations in Egypt.

 

New Partnerships, More Opportunities to Create Impact

From automotive and healthcare to education and energy, Leoden dentsu welcomed a diverse group of clients, expanding our presence across industries and markets.

 

EXEED X Leoden dentsu

A new name joins the drive

EXEED is one of the latest additions to the Leoden dentsu client portfolio, marking a significant new business win and an exciting partnership in the automotive sector. This collaboration further strengthens our presence in the category and opens the door to new creative opportunities as we begin this journey together.

 
 

Elite Dental Studio X Leoden dentsu

Something to smile about

We welcomed Elite Dental Studios to Leoden dentsu, partnering with the clinic on its brand communication to showcase its services and build stronger connections with current and prospective patients.

 
 

Queen Rania Award for Excellence in Education X Leoden dentsu

An award-worthy partnership

Leoden dentsu welcomes the Queen Rania Award for Excellence in Education to its client portfolio. Dedicated to recognizing and celebrating outstanding educators and promoting excellence in Jordan’s education sector, the Award plays an important role in inspiring positive change and raising educational standards. Through this partnership, we look forward to amplifying its mission and celebrating those making a lasting difference in education.

 
 

ASKCXO X Leoden dentsu

A new partnership takes shape

Leoden dentsu welcomes ASKCXO to its client portfolio, marking the beginning of a collaboration focused on leadership, financial expertise, and executive insights. Through this partnership, we aim to strengthen ASKCXO’s communication and amplify its voice in conversations shaping the future of business and financial leadership.

 
 

Abdali Mall X Leoden dentsu

A new destination for creativity

Leoden dentsu welcomes Abdali Mall to its client portfolio, marking an exciting new partnership in Jordan’s retail and lifestyle sector. Through a comprehensive collaboration spanning marketing strategy, creative campaigns, social media, content production, and digital advertising, we look forward to strengthening the mall’s brand presence, creating engaging experiences, and bringing its retail, dining, and entertainment offerings closer to audiences.

 
 

Vivo Energy X Leoden dentsu

A new launch, a new partnership

As Vivo Energy enters the Jordanian market, Leoden dentsu continues its partnership with the business, building on its existing relationship with Total. This transition marks a new chapter in an established collaboration, bringing fresh opportunities while maintaining the continuity of a trusted partnership.

Some launches introduce a product. Others introduce an entirely new experience.

This quarter, Leoden dentsu helped bring two new experiences to market, each opening up new possibilities within its category.

HoverUp Academy

Learning To Fly Starts on the Ground

HoverUp Academy has officially launched, offering aspiring drone pilots a dedicated space to learn, practice, and develop their flying skills. Through hands-on training and practical learning, the Academy bridges the gap between theory and real-world experience, helping future pilots build the skills and confidence to take flight.

  

Dead Sea Track

Jordan Has A New Destination for Life in the Fast Lane

Dead Sea Track launched as a purpose-built automotive venue, bringing together motorsports, driving experiences, automotive events, and brand activations in one destination.

Through its launch campaign, Leoden dentsu helped introduce the venue to Jordan’s automotive community, brands, and industry professionals, positioning Dead Sea Track as a new destination for experiences that go beyond the road.

INJAZ UAE

 

100+ Students, 99 Enterprises, One Stage

Ideas took centre stage in Dubai as INJAZ UAE brought together 100+ students for the finals of its 17th annual Company Program Competition.

Students from across the region showcased their ideas, innovations and achievements while connecting with industry leaders and partners who could help turn their ambitions into real-world opportunities.

Leoden dentsu managed both the communication and the event itself, bringing together strategy, storytelling and on-ground execution for one of the region’s major celebrations of young entrepreneurship.
